Newidle load-balance counters from /proc/schedstat, summed over all 32 scheduler
domains on the SUT, delta across the measurement window.

NOTE ON LAYOUT: mainline v6.19 emits schedstat **version 17**, not the version 15
the AL2023 6.1 kernels emit. In v17 each domain line is

    domain<N> <NAME> <cpumask>  followed by 11 counters for each of
    CPU_IDLE, CPU_NOT_IDLE, CPU_NEWLY_IDLE (33), plus 12 trailing

so the CPU_NEWLY_IDLE group starts at counter offset 22 -- not 16 as in v15,
where there are 8 counters per idle type and no domain name. Parsing v17 with
v15 offsets yields plausible-looking but wrong numbers.

Fields below are the CPU_NEWLY_IDLE group: lb_count, lb_balanced, lb_failed, lb_gained.

variant   lb_count        lb_balanced      lb_failed       tasks_pulled
bad         37,172,414      16,076,277      17,966,476            2,528
good       346,204,347     163,765,497     169,876,555           31,856
ratio             9.31x           10.19x           9.46x           12.60x

The culprit scales the newidle balance budget by the domain's average load. With
the commit present the balancer attempts newidle balancing 9.3x less often and
pulls 12.6x fewer tasks across the measurement window. This is the same direction
as the AL2023 6.1 pair, measured here across a single-commit difference.
