Newidle load-balance counters from /proc/schedstat (version 15), summed over all
32 scheduler domains on the SUT, delta across the measurement window.

Fields are the CPU_NEWLY_IDLE group: lb_count, lb_balanced, lb_failed, lb_gained.
In v15 each domain line is "domain<N> <cpumask>" followed by 8 counters for each
of CPU_IDLE, CPU_NOT_IDLE, CPU_NEWLY_IDLE, so the newidle group starts at counter
offset 16.

variant   lb_count      lb_balanced   lb_failed    tasks_pulled
bad         255,783        138,791       62,375       2,145,375
good     22,970,965     12,573,980    4,162,020     659,916,869
ratio          ~90x           ~91x         ~67x            ~308x

The culprit commit scales the newidle balance budget by the domain's average load.
On this 2-LLC part that nearly stops the balancer pulling waking tasks across LLCs
while the server is near saturation: the bad kernel attempts newidle balancing ~90x
less often and pulls ~308x fewer tasks. The tail-latency increase, and the lower
max QPS under the SLO, follow from that.
